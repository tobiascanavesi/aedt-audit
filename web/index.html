Computes the metrics defined by NYC Local Law 144 (6 RCNY § 5-300 et seq.) and the EEOC Uniform Guidelines (29 CFR § 1607.4(D)). This page is tooling, not legal advice, and not an independent bias audit — under Local Law 144 the audit itself must be conducted by an independent auditor.

Runs the aedt-audit Python package inside your browser with Pyodide. The only network requests are for that code, from jsDelivr and PyPI. No analytics, no cookies.
